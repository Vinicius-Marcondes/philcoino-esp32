# PRD-014 — Weighted extraction telemetry and dashboard

Status: CLOSED — Previously Approved 2026-07-26

## Summary

Add observation-only weighted-shot traces from firmware through the simulator
and phone, derive beverage mass flow in the app, retain traces for 90 days, and
make the extraction graph the primary Dashboard surface. The visual direction
uses Philcoino's warm palette, dense instrumentation, raw segments, and a
restrained flow area rather than copying Gaggiuino.

## Goals

- Retain and page the latest weighted extraction at 250 ms cadence.
- Recover trace pages after reconnect without overlapping requests.
- Derive causal beverage flow from scale weight and preserve real gaps.
- Store, inspect, and export historical weighted traces.
- Provide responsive portrait and mounted-landscape telemetry layouts.
- Preserve temperature history and current-weight behavior on 404 inside the
  same telemetry-chart presentation.

## Non-Goals

- Measuring physical inlet-water flow.
- Capturing Manual or timed-profile traces in v1.
- Using telemetry or app-derived flow in heater, pump, scale, or safety control.
- Claiming simulator, host, or graph results prove physical accuracy or safety.
- Visually copying Gaggiuino.

## Context

PRD-011 supplies weighted extraction, current scale state, summary history, and
summary CSV. PRD-007 supplies one-Hertz temperature history and reconnect
backfill. Neither retains the weight/temperature/pump timeline required to
inspect a weighted extraction or estimate beverage flow.

## Requirements

- Add authenticated additive `GET /api/v2/scale/trace` without changing
  existing strict payloads.
- Retain at most 320 observation samples in firmware RAM, with pages of at most
  16 samples and explicit boot/extraction/sequence continuity.
- Capture weighted profile extractions only, including the bounded post-pump
  settling tail. Use a separate zero-wait trace lock and expose skipped
  sequences as gaps.
- Implement deterministic simulator capture, pagination, fallback, settling,
  reset, and weighted-only behavior.
- Synchronize and durably commit pages on mobile; cancel on background and
  resume from the last committed cursor.
- Estimate `g/s` with causal one-second linear regression, requiring three
  continuous samples spanning at least 500 ms. Reset across identity,
  availability, null, and sequence discontinuities.
- Retain raw samples and store trace completeness as `live`, `partial`, or
  `complete` in the 90-day device-scoped repository.
- Preserve summary CSV and add per-trace CSV.
- Render shared-time upper temperature/target and lower weight/flow plots with
  raw segments, gaps, phase boundaries, settling, cutoff, cursor, and markers.
- Use the telemetry chart as the permanent Dashboard graph. Without a weighted
  trace, its upper band renders the paged thirty-second temperature/target
  history, its weight metric uses current scale state, and its lower band marks
  weight history and derived flow unavailable without synthesizing samples.
- Make history rows open trace detail and visibly identify unavailable traces.

## Implementation Overview

1. Extend the strict protocol and deterministic simulator.
2. Add a fixed firmware trace ring, zero-wait trace lock, and protected API.
3. Add mobile synchronization, persistence, flow derivation, and exports.
4. Add responsive SVG telemetry primitives and historical inspection.
5. Produce the Figma concept and complete software, target, and Human review.
