# PRD-017 — Guided temperature calibration and global offset

Status: Ready

Approved: 2026-07-30

Task-sequence amendment approved: 2026-07-30

The initial implementation through TCAL-007 retains the approved fault-at-
`135°C` raw/effective behavior below. A separately supervised TCAL-008 will
change `135°C` into an inclusive Steam target only after the owner approves the
replacement raw and effective trip boundaries above that target. The existing
physical acceptance task is resequenced to TCAL-009.

## Summary

Add a guided mobile calibration screen that lets the user heat the boiler to an
uncorrected candidate temperature, open the steam wand, and adjust the candidate
in whole-degree steps until the observed boiling point is found. Saving maps that
raw sensor value to logical `100°C` and persists one signed, firmware-authoritative
temperature offset for both Brew and Steam.

This global calibration replaces the fixed Steam-only `+5°C` correction from
PRD-003. A machine without a saved calibration uses `0°C`. Firmware continues to
own sensor validation, effective temperature, heater control, persistence,
timeouts, faults, and every fail-off path.

## Goals

- Give the user a focused, repeatable way to calibrate the boiler temperature
  scale without rebuilding or reflashing firmware.
- Apply one persisted correction consistently to Brew and Steam control,
  readiness, API state, history, simulator behavior, and mobile presentation.
- Keep calibration pending until firmware acknowledges a successful NVS save.
- Raise the Steam over-temperature limit to `135°C` effective while enforcing a
  separate `135°C` raw-sensor ceiling that an offset cannot bypass.
- Preserve firmware authority and require separate Human acceptance for
  physical calibration, thermostat interruption, and energized heater behavior.

## Non-Goals

- Automatic steam, boiling-point, altitude, pressure, or location detection.
- Automatically operating the pump or steam valve.
- Sensor gain, interpolation, correction curves, autotuning, PID tuning, or
  changing Brew/Steam targets as part of calibration.
- Making the phone part of the normal temperature-control or safety loop.
- Proving the retained thermostat's tolerance, wiring, physical interruption,
  or the accuracy of a user-observed boiling point through software tests.
- Authorizing energized testing, production use, or weakening the existing
  independent cutoff and fail-off requirements.

## Context

Philcoino currently reports and controls Brew from the raw boiler-base MAX6675
reading, while Steam adds a compile-time `+5°C` correction. PRD-003 explicitly
deferred runtime calibration and applying a correction to Brew.

[GaggiMate documents a configurable temperature offset](https://docs.gaggimate.eu/docs/usage-configuration/)
for compensating between boiler-wall and delivered-water temperature. This PRD
adopts the idea of one machine-specific offset, but not GaggiMate's exact
semantics or UI: Philcoino rebases the observed raw boiling point to logical
`100°C` through its own guided workflow.

The owner reports retaining a `145°C`, 10 A, 250 V thermostat matching this
[Britânia/Philco replacement listing](https://www.mercadolivre.com.br/termostato-da-cafeteira-expresso-145c-250v-10a-britania/up/MLBU3950409904).
The listing supports only nominal component identification. It does not prove
the installed thermostat's tolerance, series wiring, thermal coupling, or
ability to interrupt the heater.

## Requirements

### Calibration semantics and persistence

- Define the saved signed correction as:

  ```text
  temperatureOffsetC = 100 - observedRawBoilingTemperatureC
  effectiveTemperatureC = rawTemperatureC + temperatureOffsetC
  ```

- A raw boiling point of `108°C` saves `-8°C`; `95°C` saves `+5°C`; and
  `100°C` saves `0°C`.
- Use `0°C` when no calibration record exists. Remove the fixed Steam-only
  `+5°C` correction and prevent any caller from applying a second offset.
- Persist the offset atomically in firmware NVS. A failed save must retain the
  prior offset and return a rejected mutation.
- Treat a missing record as uncalibrated with `0°C`. Treat corrupt or unreadable
  calibration storage as an internal fault that commands the heater off.
- Apply the correction only after validating that the raw thermocouple sample
  is finite, plausible, and transport-valid.

### Guided calibration workflow

- Open a focused Temperature Calibration screen from the mobile Machine page.
- Start a new calibration at raw target `100°C`. A recalibration starts from the
  raw boiling point implied by the saved offset.
- Allow whole-degree candidate targets from `90°C` through `120°C`.
- During calibration, firmware controls the uncorrected raw candidate target;
  the saved offset must not influence this temporary target.
- Show raw temperature, effective-temperature preview, candidate target, offset
  preview, heater command, readiness, and advisory stable time.
- Do not gate testing or saving on a stabilization countdown and do not impose
  an overall calibration duration. The user decides when the system is stable.
- Instruct the user to open the steam wand manually and observe the result.
  Calibration must never command the pump or claim to detect physical steam.
- Save only after explicit confirmation. Cancel, navigation away, connection
  loss, reset, fault, or conflicting workflow transition discards the candidate
  and restores ordinary Brew control without changing persisted targets.
- Allow calibration only with a valid sensor, enabled heater permission, no
  fault, and no active extraction, cooldown, scale calibration, Steam workflow,
  or conflicting mutation.

### Normal control and Steam safety

- Use effective temperature for Brew and Steam control, readiness, recovery,
  heating deadlines, normal over-temperature decisions, API state, history,
  simulator state, and mobile presentation.
- Keep persisted Brew and Steam target numbers unchanged. Never rewrite or
  clamp targets as a side effect of saving calibration.
- Raise the effective Steam over-temperature threshold from `130°C` to `135°C`.
- Add a separate `135°C` raw thermocouple ceiling. Reaching either `135°C`
  effective or `135°C` raw must latch `over_temperature` and command the heater
  off.
- Evaluate the raw ceiling independently before applying the offset so positive
  or negative corrections cannot bypass it.
- Reject a calibration save when the current Steam target would require a raw
  target at or above `135°C`.
- Reject later target mutations that would be unreachable under the saved
  offset and raw ceiling. Return a strict error; never silently clamp a target.
- Fault dismissal must require both effective and raw temperatures to satisfy
  their safe return conditions.
- Preserve the independent thermostat, heater safety lease, SSR fail-off owner,
  boot-off ordering, sensor failure behavior, and existing physical-validation
  boundaries.

### Contract, simulator, and mobile

- Add strict authenticated API v2 calibration operations for status, start,
  candidate adjustment, save, and cancel.
- Return calibration status, saved offset, candidate raw target, raw and
  effective temperatures, advisory stability duration, and offset-adjusted safe
  target bounds.
- Keep existing state payload shapes compatible while updating the documented
  meaning of `boilerTemperatureC` to the firmware-authoritative effective
  temperature in both modes.
- Serialize calibration and target mutations. Mobile must display requested
  values only as drafts until a valid firmware response acknowledges them.
- Model raw temperature, effective temperature, calibration workflow,
  persistence, cancellation, and boundary rejection deterministically in the
  simulator without claiming physical calibration evidence.
- Localize the calibration screen and make its instructions, pending state,
  faults, confirmation, and cancellation accessible in supported layouts.

### Verification and Human acceptance

- Cover offset arithmetic, sign, bounds, single application, first boot,
  persistence, reboot, recalibration, cancellation, connection loss, corrupt
  storage, and failed persistence.
- Test effective `135°C` with raw below the ceiling and raw `135°C` with
  effective below the ceiling; either path must fail off.
- Verify incompatible calibration saves and later target changes are rejected,
  and that no offset can bypass the raw ceiling.
- Verify invalid sensor input, heater permission, faults, safety lease, output
  failures, cooldown, extraction, and conflicting calibration remain
  authoritative.
- Run affected OpenAPI/protocol checks, simulator tests/typecheck, mobile
  tests/typecheck/lint, firmware host/sanitizer suites, and the pinned ESP-IDF
  target build when available.
- Treat software, simulator, and target-build results as software evidence only.
  Physical steam observation, calibration accuracy, thermostat interruption,
  and energized heater behavior require explicit authorization and Human review.

## Implementation Overview

1. Define the additive API v2 calibration transaction, offset bounds, safe
   target bounds, and effective-temperature semantics in the protocol.
2. Add firmware calibration storage and workflow ownership, centralize
   raw-to-effective conversion, remove the fixed Steam correction, and enforce
   both `135°C` limits.
3. Align the deterministic simulator and mobile networking/session behavior.
4. Add the Machine-page calibration screen, acknowledged save/cancel flow,
   localization, and accessibility.
5. Complete software and target verification, then perform separately
   authorized Human calibration and thermostat-interruption acceptance.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The Machine page opens a focused calibration screen with a `100°C`
  initial raw target, `1°C` controls from `90–120°C`, live raw/effective state,
  offset preview, and advisory stability time.
- [ ] The workflow tells the user to operate the steam wand manually and never
  commands the pump or claims automatic steam detection.
- [ ] Saving maps the selected raw boiling point to logical `100°C`, persists
  the signed offset atomically, and applies it exactly once to Brew and Steam.
- [ ] A missing record is distinguishable as uncalibrated with a `0°C` default;
  an explicitly saved `0°C` remains calibrated, while corrupt or unreadable
  storage faults and commands the heater off.
- [ ] Cancel, navigation, disconnection, reset, fault, and conflicting workflow
  transitions discard unsaved calibration and restore ordinary Brew control.
- [ ] The fixed Steam-only `+5°C` correction is absent and PRD-003 is explicitly
  superseded for current temperature semantics.
- [ ] Effective Steam temperature at `135°C` or raw temperature at `135°C`
  independently latches `over_temperature` and fails off.
- [ ] Unsafe calibration saves and later unreachable target mutations are
  rejected without clamping or silently changing persisted targets.
- [ ] Existing sensor validation, heater permission, safety lease, boot-off,
  SSR fail-off, faults, workflows, and independent physical protection remain
  authoritative.
- [ ] Protocol, simulator, mobile, firmware host/sanitizer, and available pinned
  target checks pass, with unavailable evidence recorded.
- [ ] Energized calibration and verification of the retained `145°C` thermostat
  remain pending explicit authorization and Human acceptance.

## Open Questions

None for product behavior. The actual offset is a runtime Human calibration
result, not a value prescribed by this PRD. Exact energized procedures,
instrumentation, thermostat tolerance, and wiring evidence remain Human-owned
acceptance work and must be defined in supervised task planning after this PRD
is approved.
