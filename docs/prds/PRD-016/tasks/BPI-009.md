# BPI-009 — Perform supervised physical A/B acceptance

Status: Software Complete — Target/Human Pending
Review Mode: Human
Human Review Needs: Authorize and supervise the exact low-voltage/energized
machine setup, independently verify cutoff and instrumentation, review captured
legacy-versus-PI thermal evidence, and explicitly accept or reject active PI.

## Goal

Prepare and, only after separate explicit authorization, execute the physical
legacy-versus-active-PI comparison required for owner acceptance.

## Scope

- Provide a bounded procedure recording build flag, revision, gains, filter,
  SSR window, target, scenario, sensor/setup, independent cutoff, ambient/start
  conditions, requested/delivered command trace, and measured temperature.
- Compare median overshoot, recovery time, idle stability, heater faults,
  timeouts, switching, deadlines, and safety-lease behavior.
- Require explicit owner review against PRD-016 thresholds.
- Keep the task deferred when authorization, connected target, instrumentation,
  independent cutoff verification, or Human reviewer is unavailable.

## Non-Scope

- Autonomous energized testing, wiring changes, certification, installing
  tooling, inferring physical behavior from software, or weakening any safety
  mechanism to complete a test.

## Implementation Plan

1. Freeze the exact legacy and active-PI build identities and configurations.
2. Review the physical procedure, cutoff, instrumentation, and stop conditions.
3. After explicit authorization, collect matched supervised A/B sessions.
4. Calculate accepted metrics without hiding faults or excluded runs.
5. Record an explicit Human accept/reject decision for active PI.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Software preparation and evidence templates are complete.
- [ ] Physical work occurs only with explicit authorization and supervision.
- [ ] Accepted evidence shows at least 30% lower median warm-up/recovery
  overshoot and no more than 20% increased recovery time.
- [ ] Accepted evidence shows stable idle without sustained oscillation and no
  new heater fault, timeout, switching, deadline, or safety-lease regression.
- [x] Missing physical evidence remains a visible Human gate and is never
  represented as completed software acceptance.

## Verification Strategy

- Human-supervised, instrumented, matched-build physical A/B procedure defined
by PRD-016 and repository safety documentation.
